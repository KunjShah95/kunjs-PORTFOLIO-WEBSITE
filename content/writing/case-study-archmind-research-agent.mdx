ArchMind AI needed a brain that could actually *do* research rather than talk
about it. That brain is a self-planning agent that takes an open-ended request
and returns structured, cited findings.

## The problem

Internship and market research is repetitive and error-prone. Matching roles,
companies and requirements by hand wastes days and misses signal.

Generic search does not fix this. It returns forty links and leaves the
reasoning to the person holding them — deciding which three are credible, what
they agree on, what they contradict. That reasoning is the actual work, and it
is exactly the shape an agent is good at, provided the evidence it reasons over
is real.

## Four stages, two loops

**Plan** — a planner agent decomposes the request into focused sub-queries.
**Search** — search and scraping agents gather evidence.
**Read** — an extractor normalises structured fields out of noisy,
inconsistent, occasionally contradictory sources.
**Synthesize** — a writer agent produces a report grounded in that evidence.

It is a state machine rather than a linear script because the stages genuinely
loop. A sub-query that returns thin evidence has to go back to search with a
rephrased query. Two sources that contradict each other have to be flagged
before synthesis rather than averaged into mush. A linear pipeline expresses
neither.

## The one decision that mattered

Refusing to let the synthesis stage free-generate. It answers only from retrieved
evidence chunks. Free-form generation produces more fluent reports and more of
them are wrong in ways that are hard to spot.

The secondary decision was constraining the planner tightly. A loose planner is
the most expensive failure mode in a research agent: it decomposes broadly,
searches five times more than necessary, and drifts. Bounded sub-queries with
explicit acceptance criteria keep both cost and topic adherence under control.

## Showing the pipeline

Making each stage visible and steerable turned a black box into a tool people
finished using — and it made my own debugging dramatically faster, because a
failure is visible at the stage where it happened.

## The result

Deployed and interactive. Open-ended requests turn into structured, cited
findings in minutes.

**Autonomy without grounding is just confident guessing.**
